NOT enforced: posted-entry immutability, period close/lock, one-entry-per-source uniqueness, imp_total=Σcomponents, leaf-only posting (UI only).

## DESIGN TENSIONS
1. accrual_period vs entry_date cutoffs — diario+balances cut by accrual_period; libro mayor by entry_date; libro IVA by fecha. Three clocks; March close can never be cross-checked between the three reports.
2. Posting inside source transaction: fail-loud vs fail-silent inconsistently — balance/check violations abort business op (receipt dies at COMMIT; persist-cae runs AFTER CAE consumed at AFIP → posting error strands AFIP-registered invoice in emitting) while missing-role errors swallowed. Pick: outbox/queue or block with actionable messages.
3. Fiscal-only ledger ("two cashes") — accounting can never reconcile against treasury totals; internal↔fiscal cross-refs half-in half-out.
